# START HERE — IICS to IPUs KB

This folder is the home of the **IICS to IPUs Knowledge Base** — the end-to-end
explanation of how Informatica customers move from **legacy IICS licensing** (buying
named services with fixed tiers and connector entitlements) to the modern **IDMC IPU
consumption model** (one flexible pool of Informatica Processing Units spendable across
the whole portfolio). It covers **both** the licensing/commercial change **and** the
product/capability differences, and it is written to be **customer-facing** — safe to
share.

**What to open, in order:**

1. **`IICS to IPUs — Knowledge Base.md`** — the readable, full deliverable: history &
   naming (IICS → IDMC), the legacy licensing model, the IPU consumption model, how each
   service meters, **what customers are locked out of under legacy licensing**, **what
   the IPU model unlocks**, the side-by-side matrix, migration/conversion, the commercial
   model, a **move-vs-stay decision guide** (why move to IPUs vs. why wait), an
   FAQ/objection guide, and a glossary. **Open this to *use* the KB.**
2. **`sections\`** — the same content as **modular source-of-truth files** (one per
   topic, numbered 01–12). Agents improve these, then re-integrate the main doc.
3. **`sources\SOURCES.md`** — the citation registry. Every non-obvious claim traces here.
4. **`README.md`** — sourcing governance and the freshness policy. Read before you cite.
5. **`AGENTS.md`** — the **multi-agent improvement contract**: how any agent claims a
   section, researches, cites, logs, and re-integrates without colliding with others.
6. **`CHANGELOG.md`** — what changed, when, and by which role.

> **Customer-facing guardrail:** this KB explains the *model*, not any customer's
> numbers. It contains **no discount schedules or deal-specific pricing** — those
> are handled by Informatica per engagement. Unverified specifics are marked `[verify]`.

> **This KB is designed to be improved by many hands.** It is modular on purpose: the
> `sections\` files are the source of truth, the top-level doc is a built integration of
> them, and `AGENTS.md` is the protocol that lets multiple agents deepen and refresh it
> over time without stepping on each other.
